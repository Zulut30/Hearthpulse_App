HEARTHPULSE

Hearthpulse is a companion app for Hearthstone: a deck tracker, a Battlegrounds
advisor (heroes, comps, shop, trinkets, Discover picks, combat odds), an in-game
overlay and your match history.

HOW IT WORKS
- It reads the log files Hearthstone writes on your computer. On first start it
  turns on the log sections it needs in Hearthstone's log.config; restart
  Hearthstone once afterwards.
- On Windows it also reads a few values from the game's memory while
  Hearthstone runs. Read-only: it never writes to the game. That is:
  - your Battlegrounds rating (solo and duos, also right after a game);
  - in a game, both players' names and ranked medals;
  - in Battlegrounds, the lobby's tribes and the costs on the tavern
    buttons (refresh, freeze, upgrade, Dark Gift);
  - in Battlegrounds with opponent boards on, the leaderboard hero under
    your mouse, at most four times a second and only while the mouse is
    near the leaderboard;
  - in Battlegrounds with "Opponents' MMR" on (off by default), the names
    of the lobby's players, as the game shows them on its leaderboard. They
    are matched on this computer against Blizzard's public leaderboard of
    your region, which is downloaded whole once a day; the names are never
    sent, saved or written to a log;
  - in a Constructed game with the card pool on, the card under your
    mouse, at most four times a second and only while a card in your
    hand has a pool to show;
  - to find these, the names and layout of the game's classes.
- It never changes the game, never sends input to it and never shows hidden
  information such as your opponent's hand or deck.
- Your history and settings stay on this computer, in %APPDATA%\Hearthpulse.
- Moving from Hearthstone Deck Tracker or Firestone (Settings > Data): when
  you ask, it reads the games and decks those apps saved on this computer.
  It never changes their files and uses none of their code.
- Discord status (off by default): it talks only to the Discord app on this
  computer, which shows your current mode and matchup on your profile.

UPDATES
- Hearthpulse asks GitHub (github.com/Zulut30/Hearthpulse_App) for the newest
  release every 6 hours, or when you press "Check now" in Settings > About.
  Only that ordinary request is sent; the last answer is kept on this
  computer so an unchanged release is not downloaded again.
- Updates are checked by signature: a download is installed only when its
  SHA-256 matches the one GitHub publishes and, in builds that carry the
  project's update key, the release's list of checksums (SHA256SUMS.txt) is
  signed with that key (Ed25519). Anything else is not installed.
- Before the setup runs, the app folder is copied aside; if the setup fails
  twice, the copy is put back and the app tells you on its next start. Your
  history and settings are not touched. Turn automatic updates off in
  Settings > About.

GAME STATISTICS
- On by default; expanded source sending starts only after the updated in-app
  notice and a statistics endpoint are available. Turn it off in Settings >
  Data, "Send my games to Hearthpulse statistics".
- The app sends compact game records and a sanitized journal of observed games
  and Arena draft choices to Hearthpulse's private statistics service: ordered
  visible offers and choices, played/drawn cards, buys, sells, shop refreshes,
  resources, visible board states and outcomes. Incomplete observations are
  marked separately. Stored journals can be reprocessed as the parser improves.
- No name, BattleTag, account, deck name, exact rating, private network log or
  hidden opponent cards. Source identities are random per installation and
  generation, separate from the website account; only aggregate statistics
  are published, including in paid features. Storage is up to 180 days.
- Turning sending off clears pending journals and asks the server to delete
  uploaded data. Pending deletion remains visible until the server confirms it.
- The expanded source service is a synthetic pilot in this build; its default
  endpoint is unset. Public activation requires the matching published privacy
  policy and verified hosting/backup deletion procedures.

CRASH REPORTS
- Off by default. A report of an unexpected error goes to hearthpulse.net only
  when you press "Send report" in the error window, or when you turn on
  "Send crash reports automatically" in Settings > Data (turning it off deletes
  reports waiting to go).
- A report holds the app, system and .NET versions, the interface language,
  the error type and its text, and where in the code it happened. Names,
  BattleTags, e-mail addresses, paths, quoted values and long numbers are cut
  out of the text before it is sent. It is not tied to your account, and your
  IP address is not kept. Reports are kept for 90 days.

SHORTCUTS
- Ctrl+Shift+H  hide or show the overlay
- Ctrl+Shift+L  arrange overlay panels

UNINSTALL
Use "Apps & features" in Windows Settings, or run "Uninstall Hearthpulse" in the
install folder. You can choose whether to keep your match history.
